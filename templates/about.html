PROJECT OVERVIEW

Our Community Knowledge is Our Identity.

Community Knowledge Vault (CKV) is an open community platform built to document, protect, and share local wisdom, skills, and traditions across Amravati.

How the Project Started

We selected this project because useful local knowledge is often shared through personal experience and conversations but is not always recorded in one place. CKV provides a website where this information can be shared, organized and explored.

What CKV Provides

01

Share Useful Knowledge

Community members can submit step-by-step guides, farming tips, recipes, and local history.

02

Explore Community Knowledge

Search and filter knowledge entries by category, skill level, and keywords.

03

Ask Questions

Registered users can ask questions on knowledge posts and receive answers from contributors.

04

Send Learning Requests

Request 1-on-1 guidance from contributors to learn skills directly.

05

Admin Approval & Verification

Administrators review submissions to approve posts and mark verified content.
